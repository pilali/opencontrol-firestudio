OpenControl for PreSonus FireStudio

This installer sets up, on macOS 12 or later:

  • the PreSonus FireWire 4.2.1 drivers (audio and MIDI), extracted unmodified from the official Universal Control 3.4.2 installer you downloaded;
  • FireStudio Mixer, a free app that replaces FireControl / Universal Control for the original FireStudio 26x26: 9 mixes, output routing, headphones, meters, presets.

The PreSonus kext only works on Intel Macs. On an Apple Silicon Mac, select the app only.
